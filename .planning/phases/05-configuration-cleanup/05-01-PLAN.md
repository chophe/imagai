<objective>
Rewrite `imagai.config` as a pure declaration of settings: remove the manual `os.environ` parsing loop and the import-time `mkdir`, give `EngineConfig.api_key` a `"dummy"` default, and document engine names containing `__` as unsupported. Prove import purity with a fresh-process test and env-parity with a parametrized test over every `.env.example` engine.

**MVP user story:** As a developer, I want `imagai.config` to be a pure declaration of settings so that importing it has no side effects and pydantic-settings is the single source of truth for engine configuration.

Purpose: Eliminate the two import-time side effects (directory creation and global state mutation) so that importing `imagai.config` is safe in any context, and make pydantic-settings the single source of engine configuration.
Output: A pure `config.py`, a new `tests/test_config.py` with import-purity and env-parity tests.
</objective>
